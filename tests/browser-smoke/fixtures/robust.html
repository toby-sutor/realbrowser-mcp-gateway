The market square

ROBUSTVIS-MAIN On Saturdays the square fills before seven. The cheese stall stands where it has stood for forty years, between the fountain and the steps of the town hall, and the woman who runs it knows most of her customers by the bread they carry.

By noon the crates are stacked again and the pigeons have the cobbles to themselves. The sweeper comes at one, and by two there is nothing left to show that anything was sold here at all. ROBUSTVIS-END
