The lighthouse at the point

EDGEVIS-MAIN The lighthouse was built in 1871 after two ships were lost on the reef in one winter. Its lamp turns once in twenty seconds and is seen for nineteen miles; the keeper's house beside it has been empty since the light was made automatic.
