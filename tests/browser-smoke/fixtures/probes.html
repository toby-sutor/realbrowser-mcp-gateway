Notes on keeping bees through the winter

PROBEVIS-PLAIN A colony goes into winter as a cluster of perhaps ten thousand bees around its stores. The beekeeper's work is done by October; what follows is mostly a matter of leaving the hive alone and reading what can be read from the outside.
