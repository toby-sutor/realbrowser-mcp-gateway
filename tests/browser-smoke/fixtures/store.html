How tea is made

STOREVIS-MAIN Tea is made by pouring hot water over the cured leaves of the tea plant. It is the most widely drunk beverage after water, and it has been grown in China for several thousand years.

Green tea is not oxidised at all, black tea fully, and oolong lies between the two. The leaves are picked by hand in most gardens, and the first flush of the year is the most sought after. STOREVIS-END
