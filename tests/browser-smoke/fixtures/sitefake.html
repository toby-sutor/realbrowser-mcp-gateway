The harbour ferry returns

FAKEVIS-MAIN After two winters in dry dock the small ferry is crossing the harbour again. It runs every twenty minutes from the fish market to the old customs house, and the first week is free for everyone who lives on the east bank.

The council paid for a new engine and for a ramp that takes wheelchairs and prams. The crew of three is the same as before, and so is the bell that is rung when the boat casts off. FAKEVIS-END
