Harbor council approves the Quayside tram extension

By Marta Okonkwo - March 14, 2026

The harbor council voted nine to four on Thursday evening to approve the Quayside tram extension, ending three years of argument over routing, cost and the future of the old fish market. ZEBRACORP-MAIN the decision lets construction begin in the autumn.

What the vote means

The extension adds 2.4 kilometres of track and four stops. According to the published transit plan, the line will carry an estimated 18,000 passengers a day once it opens in 2029, relieving pressure on the two bus corridors that currently serve the district.

Opponents argued that the budget of 212 million had grown by a third since the first estimate. Councillor Idris Valdez said the figures were still too soft, while supporters pointed to a fixed-price agreement signed with the contractor last month. Read the full budget document for the line-by-line breakdown.

Next steps

Utility relocation starts in September, and the council will publish a detailed traffic management plan before any roads are closed. Residents can follow progress on the project page, and a public meeting is planned for the first week of October at the harbor library.

  • Utility relocation: September
  • Track laying: spring 2027
  • Opening: 2029
