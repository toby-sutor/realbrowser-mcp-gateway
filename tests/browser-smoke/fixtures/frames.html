The ferry timetable

FRAMESVIS-MAIN The first boat leaves the fish market at six and the last one at half past ten in the evening. Between seven and nine in the morning it runs every ten minutes, at other times every twenty, and on Sundays it starts an hour later.

Bicycles travel free outside the morning rush. A single ticket is valid for an hour and can be bought on board, and children under six need none at all. FRAMESVIS-END
