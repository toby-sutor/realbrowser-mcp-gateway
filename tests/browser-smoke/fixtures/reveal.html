Field notes on bringing a river back

REVEAL-INTRO For sixty years the lower reach of the river ran in a straight concrete channel. This is an account of what happened in the three summers after the channel walls were taken out, written from the notebooks kept on site.

The first summer

REVEAL-ONE Within weeks the water had cut itself a shallow bend where the old wall had stood. Gravel bars appeared below it, and by August the first sand martins were nesting in the freshly eroded bank.

The second summer

REVEAL-TWO Willow and alder seeded themselves along the new margins without any planting. The fish survey in June counted eleven species where the channel had held four, among them young grayling for the first time in living memory.

The third summer

REVEAL-THREE A flood in the spring spread over the reconnected meadow instead of rushing downstream, and the gauge in the town below recorded a peak a third lower than the model for the old channel predicted.

What it cost

REVEAL-ANIMATED The whole scheme cost less than one year of dredging and wall repairs had, and the maintenance budget for the reach is now close to nothing.
