The lighthouse keeper's log

UMDVIS-MAIN The lamp was lit at a quarter past five and burned until the fog lifted the next morning. Three trawlers passed the point before midnight, the last of them so close that the keeper could read the name on her bow by the light of his own lantern.

In the morning the glass was wiped, the wick trimmed and the oil measured: a little under four pints had gone. The entry was signed, and the book put back on its shelf beside the barometer. UMDVIS-END
